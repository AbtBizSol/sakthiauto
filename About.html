About Us

Sakthi Auto Component Limited is one of the influential and a trusted supplier of the safety critical components needed for passenger and commercial automobiles.

SAKTHI AUTO COMPONENT LIMITED stands out in the manufacturing of safety-critical components with a significant share in the Passenger Vehicle (PV) and Commercial Vehicle (CV) segments as the most trusted supplier. We are the GLOBAL MARKET LEADER, with a standing 90% market share in Steering Knuckles as a niche expertise.

We maintain advanced technological foundry infrastructure backed by one of the largest in the industry through Sakthi Group proprietary affiliations which align with its core belief of “honing”. The intense and symbiotic association with the automotive industry has shaped us to be a market leader that forms trends that reverberate throughout the landscape, our meteoric rise with reciprocal collaborations and harmonised trend-setting naming us as a “game-changer ” with critically acclaimed entities such as MARUTHI SUZUKI, HYUNDAI, FORD, GENERAL MOTORS, TOYOTA, VOLKSWAGEN, VOLVO, RENAULT NISSAN, STELLANTIS, MAHINDRA & MAHINDRA, ASHOK LEYLAND, KIA, ILJIN and so on.

SAKTHI AUTO COMPONENT LIMITED is located on the NH 544 - Kochi - Salem Highway in the state of Tamil Nadu in India and functions as an INTEGRATED WORKING COMPLEX STRUCTURE over 120 acres of land with unparalleled manufacturing infrastructure and amenities. Since our opening in 1983, we have achieved the projections to optimally produce 75,000 tons of castings and supply globally.

Using systematic transfer and collaboration from the M/s. George Fisher Foundry Systems, we have grown to greater heights in automotive parts manufacturing and continue exploring new possibilities using the industrial know-how gained. We are at the forefront of technological innovation in the automotive industry especially the auto ancillary sector that houses state-of-the-art DISAMATIC FOUNDRY Facilities with the latest MOULDING Lines that are unmatched in precision and productivity. With the partnership with M/s DISA Technologies - Denmark, we have the operations to produce 10,000 units with superior consistency and 440 Moulds per hour leading to new global efficiency benchmarks.Our Automatic Pouring unit is sourced from M/s. ASEA BROWN BOVERI (ABB) Limited, a renowned Swedish-Swiss automation major, ensures round-the-clock temperature control that guarantees seamless manufacturing process. Vision and mission extends beyond just automotive parts but to raise the name “SAKTHI” to be synonymous with engineering and mechanism finesse in meeting global standards.

We are strongly committed to quality in manufacturing which is very much visible in our Research & Development faculty & facilities. The high involvement of the research and development(R&D) team in all aspects of SACL has created a renaissance within the organization. We have cutting-edge R&D testing technologies and tools, state of art industrial metrology laboratory (accredited by the NABL, INDIA) to make our products comply with the stringent quality norms globally.

Our advanced surface treatment facilities such as coating facilities, including Powder coating, Phosphating, and ED coating cater to the needs of our customers and gear ourselves towards to stand on par with international facilities. At SACL, we relentlessly pursue international distinction threaded with quality assurance and engineered reliability. These are embedded deeply into us, setting us to be the unanimous choice for automakers. We have always understood the impact that our business has on individuals and communities. Our organisation is and will continue to impact the lives of every single individual we touch upon.
