Plant & Machinery


Our advanced surface coating facility is designed to enhance the durability, corrosion resistance, and aesthetic finish of cast and machined components as per the customer requirement.

  • Semi-Automatic Dip Painting Process
  • Phosphating Process
  • Spray Painting Process
  • Powder Coating Process
  • Cathodic Electro Deposition (CED) Coating

Spray Painting Process

Our spray-painting operations combine robotic systems and conventional spray booths equipped with high-efficiency guns and advanced air handling systems. This hybrid setup ensures uniform coating, smooth surface finishes, and optimum paint transfer efficiency. It is especially suited for components that demand both aesthetic appeal and robust corrosion protection.
